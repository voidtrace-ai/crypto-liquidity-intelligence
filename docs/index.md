## Overview

VOIDTRACE AI is a blockchain intelligence software concept designed to organize and interpret cross-chain market activity. Combines specialized AI agents with blockchain data to analyze liquidity flows, stablecoin movements, capital rotation, DEX activity, bridge activity, and ecosystem momentum.

---

## Intelligence Signals

| Signal | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| liquidity-flow | Cross-chain liquidity movement and capital flow analysis |
| stablecoin-intel | Stablecoin supply, movement, and allocation intelligence |
| capital-rotation | Capital rotation patterns between chains and protocols |
| dex-activity | DEX volume, depth, and trading activity signals |
| bridge-activity | Cross-chain bridge flows and connectivity analysis |
| ecosystem-momentum | Blockchain ecosystem momentum and growth scoring |

---

## Supported Chains

| Chain | Coverage |
|-------|---------|
| Ethereum | Full liquidity, DEX, bridge, and stablecoin coverage |
| BNB Chain | DeFi activity, bridge flows, and stablecoin signals |

| Optimism | L2 ecosystem momentum and bridge activity |
| Polygon | Cross-chain bridge and DEX activity signals |
| Avalanche | Ecosystem momentum and capital rotation |
| Base | Emerging L2 liquidity and DEX activity signals |
